One platform, three pillars

Everything an IONM service needs, working as one system.

From the first baseline to the signed report, NeuroIONM keeps clinical context, signals, and knowledge connected — so teams act faster and document better.

IONM Live

An on-job operating surface that organizes the case around the right clinical context and surfaces the next right action.

  • Procedure templates with channel & electrode suggestions
  • Calibrated, editable body-map coordinates
  • Baseline capture and real-time event timeline
  • Technical, anesthesia & surgical differential checklists

IONM Academy

A structured learning environment that turns daily practice into measurable competency.

  • Foundations through advanced modality tracks
  • Case scenarios, quizzes and anatomy/protocol training
  • Progress and competency tracking per learner
  • Shared knowledge that mirrors the live reference

IONM Intelligence

AI assistance grounded in your own curated literature — answers come with the evidence behind them.

  • PDF/OCR knowledge ingestion into a private RAG index
  • Troubleshooting suggestions with cited sources
  • Admin-curated, organization-specific knowledge base
  • No black boxes — every recommendation is traceable
Clinical coverage

Built around the modalities your cases actually demand.

NeuroIONM models the real decision tree — region to root to muscle to electrode — across the full breadth of intraoperative monitoring.

Spine

Cervical, thoracic and lumbar — disc levels and nerve roots with mapped muscle and electrode coverage.

Cranial nerves & ENT

Procedure templates and individual CN III–XII workups for skull base, posterior fossa and parotid surgery.

SSEP

Median, ulnar, posterior tibial and common peroneal pathways from stimulation to interpretation criteria.

Pedicle screws

Cervical and lumbar stimulation thresholds with structured pitfall and interpretation guidance.

Thyroid / RLN

RLN and eSLN setup with vagus baseline and V1/R1/R2/V2 loss-of-signal interpretation.

MEP & EMG

Free-running and triggered EMG with motor pathway monitoring woven into the live timeline.

How it works

A clean line from setup to signed report.

Plan

Create the case, pick the procedure template, side, levels and modalities — channels are proposed for you.

Monitor

Capture baselines and track events on a live timeline with calibrated body-map context.

Interpret

Get alert-criteria guidance and AI troubleshooting backed by cited evidence the moment signals change.

Report

Generate a structured final report that captures the decisions, timeline and outcome of the case.

Deployment & control

Your data, your instance, your credentials.

Every organization runs on an isolated deployment — its own database, its own subdomain, its own administrator. We hand it over; you own it.

Isolated multi-tenancy

A dedicated database and application instance per organization — no shared records, ever.

Self-service credentials

Your administrator changes their own username and password at any time — instantly, with no downtime.

Role-based access

A clear owner → admin → user hierarchy, where every admin inherits the capabilities of the roles beneath them.

Remote oversight

Secure real-time telemonitoring lets senior staff support cases from anywhere they are needed.
